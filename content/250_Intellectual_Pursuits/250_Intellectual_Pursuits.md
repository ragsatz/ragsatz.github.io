# 🧠 Intellectual Pursuits

> [!quote] 
> "The mind is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled."
> 	 — Plutarch

This domain is the "Library of Thought." It is where we study the fundamental nature of knowledge, reality, and existence to better navigate the technical and physical worlds.

---

## 🏛️ Philosophy
The study of wisdom and the "Good Life."
* **[[Stoicism]]**: Practical ethics, the dichotomy of control, and resilience.
* **[[Ethics]]**: Frameworks for decision-making and moral consistency.
* **[[Epistemology]]**: How we know what we know.

## 🧩 Logic & Mental Models
Tools for sharper thinking and bias reduction.
* **[[211_Mental_Models]]**: A collection of reusable frameworks for problem-solving.
* **[[First Principles]]**: Deconstructing ideas to their fundamental truths.
* **[[Systems Thinking]]**: Understanding how parts interact within a whole.

## 🧬 Science & Theory
Abstract scientific concepts and high-level research.
* **[[Physics]]**: The fundamental laws governing our reality.
* **[[Complexity Theory]]**: Studying emergent behavior in chaotic systems.
