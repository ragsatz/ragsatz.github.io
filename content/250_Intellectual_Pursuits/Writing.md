## The Spark
> Why should I write? What is the incentive here? Why would someone read what I find interesting enough to write about? Do I even write good?

### Everyone should write

- I think writing can serve as a very effective way of externalizing thinking. We can use logic and rigor with our internal thoughts and find patterns or find solutions that were hitherto hidden.
- Writing is one of most fundamental skills and a very useful skill a human can possess, one can use it for a number of tasks like persuasion, reasoning, effective communication of emotions, rallying support for a cause, etc
- Every human has a different experience in the world, and there must be many with whom you have some overlap in terms of shared experiences. Even if one person reads my writings,  and find it relatable, useful or even amusing, I would feel happy.
- It's a way to connect with yourself, it feels like a form of meditation, thoughts flow continuously, smoothly into one another and leads to new discoveries about oneself and the world one lives in
- It's a good way of organising your life, your problems, your plans, your progress and everything else.
- It's fun (for me at least). I feel heard. 

> [!quote] "Paper has more patience than the people." 
> — *Anne Frank*
