## The Spark
> I find myself thinking about whether to participate in the game of life and get obsessed with winning and leveling up or withdraw and try to find happiness and satisfaction with less

- I was trying to find me some books to add to my list ([[Readings]]), I found myself confused as I was being drawn towards two opposing paradigms(seemingly opposing)
	- Detachment from the world - Spirituality, higher purpose, meditation
	- Leveling up by playing the game of life - Self improvement
- So there are two distinct paths, one is detachment from the competitions, rat-race, world, etc
	- Going towards enlightenment, pursuit of happiness and joy withing ourselves, like Buddha
	- Second is optimizing everything in your life to the perfect balance to increase your strengths, decrease your weaknesses and getting ahead or succeeding in material terms
- Observation: Why is it always a dichotomy for me? Everything doesn't need to be high-stakes choice from non-overlapping options. Why am I trying to optimize everything on paper and decide my entire life in one go? I have a tendency to do that.
