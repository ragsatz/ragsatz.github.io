## The Spark
> How can I recover from failure without feeling sad and guilty or hypercritical of oneself?

A few questions to ask oneself:
- Why do we need success?
	- Because I feel happy and content with myself when I work and put efforts in creating my successful identity and leveling up
	- I feel bad and stressed when I feel like I am degenerating, by not doing what I should in accordance with my long term plans and vision of a successful life
- 
- Why did I fail?
	- I decided to act a certain way
	- I failed to act that way
	- Why did it happen?
		- Because a human is not a machine that you can just program in a single go
		- We get bored by doing the same things over and over again
		- We need novelty so that we don't get bored
		- We did not overcome the excuses or traps laid out by our brain 
			- Why does our brain trick us to go against our own long term plans?
				- Because it perceives that the 'good' path is hard?
				- It doesn't want to do hard things
				- Brain is an efficient and lazy system, which just priorities familiarity and comfort (short-term) over long term goals
				- We have certain neural pathways in our brain that get activated by certain triggers which could be subconscious thoughts, physical phenomena, or literally anything
					- The more we repeat an action ,the easier it gets for our brain to repeat it, that's how conditioning works and habits are formed in a similar way
					- But sometimes, even when we are familiar with certain tasks, we don't really wanna do it, do we?
						- For example, you are familiar with some mundane task like creating your weekly progress report but you just don't enjoy doing it
							- Because there is no neurological reward at the end of that action, strong enough to induce cravings for that action
					- How do we weaken detrimental but highly rewarding pathways in our brain that are roadblocks in our long term vision?
						- OBSERVE and RECOGNIZE the traps, excuses of your brain, at the start of the neurological cycle
							- [[INCREASE SELF-AWARENESS]]
						- Have a STRONG DRIVE towards your long term goals, that deter you from engaging in those cycles
							- [[CREATE A STRONG DRIVE]]
				- Our brain doesn't perceive the actual cost of the 'bad' action(or failing to act a certain way)
					- It thinks it's a free lunch,  good feelings at a very cheap short-term cost
					- It has no perception or less perception of long-term effects of the aforementioned action
- How to learn from failures?
	- Analyze the failure
	- Come up with a better system
- Maybe your definition of success is wrong?
	- Have we defined success as not failing even a single time?
		- It is even feasible?
