## The Spark
> Inspired by Solo Leveling, Isn't it a powerful mental model to view life as different spheres where you can level up by doing some actions repeatedly?

### Life as a Game
- Life can be viewed as a game where the objective can differ from person to person. Evolution says the objective of the game of life is to survive for as long as possible. But I think, it's a search of meaning and attempting to fulfill that perceived meaning that's the modern objective as survival has greatly improved with the advent of modern amenities and healthcare.
- Everyone is dealt some cards at the beginning. You cannot control what variables you are initiated with, when you are born.  You can only optimize whatever you have and improve that and utilize that to achieve your objective.
- Can we use concepts from [[Game Theory]] to level up in our life?
- CRUCIAL LIMITATION: You cannot take different branches in life like you can in a VN or a game. You cannot always retry levels so you have to be extra careful.
